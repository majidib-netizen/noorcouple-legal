Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Synchronize duo mode with your spouse — legal basis: performance of the contract, upon your voluntary activation

4. Duo Mode and Encryption

In duo mode, your answers to questions are encrypted on your phone before being sent to our servers, using a key derived from your couple's unique duo code. This mechanism is designed to limit the exposure of your content. The publisher does not access the content of your answers in the normal course of operating the service. However, as no technical system can guarantee absolute confidentiality, we invite you not to record information you would consider particularly sensitive.

5. Data Recipients

Your personal data may be transmitted to the following sub-processors, each acting under its own terms and security policy:

  • Supabase (database hosting, servers located in Germany): email, first name, age, couple data, duo code, personalized plan, answers to questions and challenges.
  • RevenueCat (technical subscription management): user identifier, subscription status, purchase receipts transmitted by Apple/Google. RevenueCat has no access to couple data or answers.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play Store (payment): full payment processing according to their own policies. We do not receive or store any banking data.
  • Expo (push notifications): anonymous notification token.

Each sub-processor remains responsible for the security of its own systems in accordance with GDPR. The publisher cannot be held liable for outages, breaches, or incidents attributable to its sub-processors, except in cases of proven fault in their selection.

Your data is never sold, rented, or transferred to third parties for advertising purposes.
