Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

NoorCouple is a mobile application published by Majid Ibourahene (self-employed), designed for Muslim couples to help strengthen their bond through a personalized 40-day journey, daily questions, and a duo mode.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is:

  • Name: Majid Ibourahene
  • Status: Self-employed
  • Email: contact.casquedev@gmail.com
  • Application: NoorCouple

2. Data Collected

NoorCouple collects only data strictly necessary for the application to function:

2.1 Data provided by the user

  • Email address (for account creation)
  • First name
  • Gender (male/female) — required to tailor advice according to Islamic tradition
  • Password (stored encrypted and irreversibly by Supabase)
  • Answers to daily questions (encrypted before transmission)
  • 40-day plan diagnostic results

2.2 Technical data

  • Unique device identifier (for push notifications)
  • User preferences (language, notification times)
  • Application progress (validated days, completed challenges)

2.3 Data NOT collected

NoorCouple does NOT collect:

  • Your contacts
  • Your location
  • Your photos or media
  • Your browsing history
  • Your banking information (managed exclusively by Apple Store and Google Play)

3. Purposes of Processing

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Create and manage your user account
  • Personalize your experience based on your gender and diagnostic
  • Synchronize duo mode with your spouse
  • Send you notifications for your daily actions
  • Improve the application's functionality
  • Contact you in case of technical issues

4. Duo Mode and Encryption

In duo mode, your answers to questions are encrypted with a code unique to your couple before being stored on our servers. This encryption protects your data against unauthorized third-party access. The publisher commits to not accessing the content of your responses during normal use of the service.

5. Subprocessors

NoorCouple uses the following services:

  • Supabase (database hosting and authentication) — Servers in Europe (Germany)
  • Apple App Store (iOS distribution and payments)
  • Google Play Store (Android distribution and payments)
  • Expo (push notifications)

6. Data Retention

  • Active account: as long as you use the application
  • Inactive account: 3 years after last login, automatic deletion
  • Deletion requested: within 30 days of your request

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access to your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at: contact.casquedev@gmail.com

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all communications
  • Password encryption (bcrypt)
  • Encryption of duo mode responses (XOR with unique code)
  • Limited and logged server access
  • Regular backups

9. Cookies and Trackers

NoorCouple does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. Only a local technical identifier is stored on your device to remember your preferences.

10. Minors

NoorCouple is strictly reserved for adults (18 years and older). We do not knowingly collect data about minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us immediately.

11. International Transfers

Your data is primarily hosted in Europe. Some subprocessors (Apple, Google) may transfer data to the United States under the Data Privacy Framework.

12. Policy Changes

This policy may be modified at any time. Major changes will be notified through the application. The most recent version is always available on this page.

13. Contact

For any questions regarding this policy or data processing:
